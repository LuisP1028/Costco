Section 01 · Executive Summary

Costco Wholesale Corporation (COST): Consolidated Revenue & Operating Profit Architecture

In Fiscal Year 2024 (52 weeks ended September 1, 2024), Costco Wholesale Corporation generated $254,453M in consolidated total revenue across 890 warehouse clubs (703 owned land and building, 187 leased) and e-commerce operations. By enforcing a disciplined 10.92% gross margin cap (Gross Margin Ceiling) on $249,625M in merchandise Net Sales (98.10% of revenue) and financing 104.15% of merchandise inventory through trade accounts payable, Costco covers warehouse SG&A expenses (9.14% of net sales) from merchandise volume alone—allowing $4,828M in annual Membership Fees (1.90% of revenue) to represent 52.00% of consolidated operating income ($9,285M) and generate $7,367M in net income.

Section 03 · Five-Step Revenue Model

How Costco Generates Revenue in 5 Steps

Costco's business model links annual membership fees directly to high-volume, low-margin merchandise operations. Use the interactive stepper below (or scroll through Steps 1 to 5) to trace how annual membership fees, concentrated purchasing across fewer than 4,000 SKUs, frequent repeat visits from gas stations and ancillary services, a 10.92% gross margin cap, and supplier-financed inventory combine to generate $9,285M in operating income and $7,367M in net income.

STEP 01

Paid Membership Access and Executive Tier Concentration ($4,828M Membership Fees)

$4,828M Membership Fees (1.90% of Revenue)

Every dollar of Costco revenue begins at the warehouse entrance. Unlike open-access retailers, Costco requires households and businesses to purchase an annual paid membership before accessing any physical club or member-only e-commerce pricing. Under ASC 606 (Note 1, p. 46), membership fees are collected in cash upfront, recorded on the Consolidated Balance Sheet as Deferred membership fees ($2,501M at FY2024 end), and recognized ratably (1/12th per month) over the 12-month membership term.

  • Paid Cohort & Cardholder Scale (Item 1, p. 5): 76.2M total paid members (63.7M Gold Star + 12.5M Business, +7.32% YoY) plus 60.6M household cards equal 136.8M total cardholders worldwide.
  • Executive Tier Concentration (73.3% of Worldwide Sales): 35.4M paid Executive members represent 46.5% of the paid member base yet generate 73.3% of worldwide Net Sales. Their 2% annual reward ($2,804M in FY2024; $2,435M Accrued member rewards liability) is accounted for as a direct contra-revenue reduction against Net Sales, never as an SG&A expense.
  • Industry-Leading Renewal Rates & Sept 1, 2024 Fee Increase (Item 7, p. 25): Trailing 7-to-18-month renewal reached 92.9% in the U.S. & Canada and 90.5% worldwide. Effective September 1, 2024, U.S./Canada fees rose from $60 to $65 (Gold Star/Business) and $120 to $130 (Executive, reward cap $1,000→$1,250), adding an estimated +$370M over FY2025–FY2026 (~$190M in FY2025).

STEP 02

High-Volume Purchasing Across Fewer Than 4,000 SKUs ($249,625M Net Sales)

$249,625M Net Sales (98.10% of Revenue)

Once inside the club, members encounter Costco's second structural engine: concentrated SKU selection. Whereas traditional supermarkets and big-box retailers stock 30,000 to 100,000+ SKUs, a Costco warehouse carries fewer than 4,000 active SKUs across 890 warehouses (130.9M sq ft). Concentrating $249,625M of Net Sales into <4,000 items channels >$62M of annual purchasing volume into each average SKU, giving Costco substantial supplier negotiating scale.

  • Note 11 Merchandise Category Mix (p. 61): Foods and Sundries $101,463M (40.65%, +5.50% YoY) anchors weekly grocery replenishment; Non-Foods $63,973M (25.63%, +5.11% YoY) covers appliances, electronics, and rotating seasonal and discretionary general merchandise; Warehouse Ancillary & Other $49,969M (20.02%, +2.62% YoY) drives repeat member visits; and Fresh Foods $34,220M (13.71%, +7.01% YoY) drives perishable inventory turnover.
  • Kirkland Signature Private-Label Operations (Item 1, p. 6): Spanning ~30%+ of sales and backed by dedicated meat, bakery, optical, and coffee roastery facilities, Kirkland Signature items are priced 15%–20% below national brands while earning higher gross margin bps (contributing a +36 bps core merchandise margin improvement in FY2024).
  • Cross-Docked Depot Pallet Architecture (Item 2, p. 17): Vendors ship full pallets through 31.9M sq ft of cross-docking depots directly onto warehouse concrete floors, reducing handling labor and inventory shrinkage.

STEP 03

Ancillary Businesses and E-Commerce Fulfillment ($49,969M Ancillary + ~7% E-Commerce)

$49,969M Ancillary (20.02%) + ~7% E-Commerce

To ensure members realize consistent savings on everyday purchases that more than offset their annual membership fee, Costco pairs its core merchandise aisles with high-volume Warehouse Ancillary businesses ($49,969M or 20.02% of FY2024 Net Sales) and e-commerce fulfillment channels that expand member spending without adding warehouse floor SKUs.

  • 719 Member-Only Gas Stations (~12% of Total Net Sales / ~$30.0B): Operating across all countries except Korea and Sweden, Costco fuel stations grew physical gallon volume +1% (+$400M) in FY2024, generating frequent repeat visits to warehouse locations each week.
  • Pharmacy, Optical, Hearing Aids, Tire & Food Court: Pharmacy led ancillary dollar growth in FY2024 (+3% YoY, +$1,276M), while Optical, Hearing Aid testing, Tire Installation, and the $1.50 Hot Dog & Soda Food Court support member retention (+19 bps ancillary & e-commerce gross margin contribution).
  • Omnichannel E-Commerce, Costco Logistics & Citi Visa Program: E-commerce (9,000–10,000 online SKUs) represented ~7% of Net Sales (~$17.5B) and grew +16% on a comparable basis in FY2024, while total Digitally Originated Sales reached ~9% (~$22.5B) alongside Costco Logistics (big-and-bulky delivery), Costco Next, Costco Travel, and Citibank co-branded Visa royalties & February reward certificates (Note 1, p. 47).

STEP 04

The 10.92% Gross Margin Cap and Supplier-Financed Inventory (104.15% Accounts Payable to Inventory)

10.92% Gross Margin Ceiling · 104.15% AP/Inventory

Conventional retailers attempt to maximize gross margin percentage (25%–40%+). Costco does the exact opposite: it enforces a structural Gross Margin Ceiling (~10.5%–11.0%), returning every dollar of vendor volume rebates (recorded as reductions to Merchandise costs per Note 1, p. 47) and supply-chain savings directly to members via lower shelf prices.

  • FY2024 Merchandise Gross Margin (10.92%): Subtracting $222,358M of Merchandise costs from $249,625M of Net Sales yields $27,267M in Merchandise Gross Margin, or 10.9232% (10.92%)—up +41 bps from 10.51% in FY2023 while remaining strictly inside the [10.0%, 11.5%] ceiling.
  • Low 9.14% SG&A Expense ($22,810M): High sales per warehouse ($285.9M/club), pallet-floor display, no commissioned salesforce, and owning the land and building at 703 of 890 warehouses (plus 134 land leases where Costco owns the building = 837 owned buildings) keep SG&A (inclusive of preopening costs per Note 1) at 9.14% of Net Sales.
  • 104.15% Supplier-Financed Inventory Float (Item 8, p. 37): Because inventory turns 12.60x per year (~28.97 Days Inventory Outstanding vs. ~31.88 Days Payable Outstanding), Accounts payable ($19,421M) divided by Merchandise inventories ($18,647M) equals 104.15% (104.1508%). Trade suppliers finance 100% of Costco's floor inventory plus +$774M in net positive cash float.

STEP 05

Operating Profit Conversion ($9,285M Operating Income to $7,367M Net Income)

$9,285M Operating Income → $7,367M Net Income

In the final step of the operating model, Costco's two revenue streams combine on the Consolidated Statement of Income (Item 8, p. 35). Because the 10.92% merchandise gross margin ($27,267M) exceeds 9.14% SG&A expenses ($22,810M), core warehouse merchandise operations generate a positive $4,457M Net Merchandise Operating Contribution (48.00% of operating income, or 1.79% of Net Sales), allowing 100% of Membership Fees ($4,828M) to flow directly into operating income.

  • Majority Operating Profit from Membership Fees (52.00%): Despite representing 1.90% (1.8974%) of consolidated revenue, $4,828M in Membership Fees account for 52.00% (51.9978%) of Costco's $9,285M consolidated operating income (3.65% operating margin).
  • Positive Net Interest Float (+$455M Net): Aided by supplier-funded inventory float ($19,421M AP) and upfront deferred membership cash ($2,501M), Costco earned $624M in Interest income and other, net against only $169M in Interest expense, lifting Income before taxes to $9,740M.
  • Bottom-Line Net Income ($7,367M): After a $2,373M provision for income taxes (24.36% effective tax rate), Costco delivered $7,367M in Consolidated Net Income ($16.56 diluted EPS, +16.95% YoY).
